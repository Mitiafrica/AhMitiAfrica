🌳 Carapa procera

Discover the botanical characteristics, traditional knowledge, scientific research and conservation of Carapa procera.

Scientific Name

Carapa procera

Family

Meliaceae

Common Names

English: African Crabwood
French: Carapa
Swahili: Carapa (regional names vary)

🌱 Botanical Description

Carapa procera is a large tropical tree belonging to the Meliaceae family. It can grow up to 30 meters tall and produces large woody fruits containing oil-rich seeds. The tree thrives in humid forests, riverbanks and woodland ecosystems.

🌍 Distribution

Carapa procera is found in many parts of tropical Africa, including Senegal, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of Congo, Uganda, Tanzania and other regions with suitable climates.

🧪 Main Active Compounds

  • Limonoids
  • Triterpenoids
  • Fatty acids
  • Phenolic compounds
  • Natural antioxidants

🌿 Traditional Uses

  • Seed oil is traditionally applied to the skin.
  • Used in some communities for massage and skin care.
  • Traditionally used in soap and cosmetic preparations.
  • Wood is valued for furniture and construction.
  • Different communities have additional traditional uses that vary by region.

🔬 Scientific Research

Researchers have studied Carapa procera seed oil for its chemical composition and biological properties. Laboratory studies have investigated antioxidant and antimicrobial activity, but more clinical research is needed before drawing conclusions about medical effectiveness in humans.
