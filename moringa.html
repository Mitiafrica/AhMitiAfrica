🌿 Moringa (Moringa oleifera)

Discover the nutritional value, traditional uses, scientific research, and safety information about Moringa oleifera.

Scientific Name

Moringa oleifera

Family

Moringaceae

Common Names

English: Moringa, Drumstick Tree
Swahili: Mlonge
French: Moringa

🌱 Botanical Description

Moringa oleifera is a fast-growing deciduous tree native to the Indian subcontinent and widely cultivated across Africa. It usually grows between 5 and 12 meters tall. Nearly every part of the plant, including the leaves, seeds, flowers, pods and roots, has traditional uses.

🌍 Distribution

Moringa is cultivated throughout tropical and subtropical regions, including many African countries such as Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, Ethiopia and Nigeria.

🧪 Main Nutrients and Active Compounds

  • Vitamin A
  • Vitamin C
  • Vitamin E
  • Calcium
  • Potassium
  • Iron
  • Protein
  • Flavonoids
  • Polyphenols

🌿 Traditional Uses

  • Leaves are commonly eaten as a nutritious food.
  • Seeds are traditionally used for water purification in some communities.
  • Leaves and pods are used in traditional cooking.
  • Various parts of the plant have traditional medicinal uses in different cultures.

🔬 Scientific Research

Scientific studies suggest that Moringa contains many nutrients and bioactive compounds with antioxidant properties. Research is continuing to evaluate its potential health effects. More high-quality clinical studies are needed for many traditional medicinal claims.

⚠️ Safety Information

  • Moringa leaves are commonly consumed as food in many regions.
  • Use supplements according to product instructions.
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding women and people taking medications should consult a qualified healthcare professional before using Moringa supplements.
  • Moringa should not replace prescribed medical treatment.
