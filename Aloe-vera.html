🌿 Aloe Vera (Aloe vera)

Discover the botanical characteristics, traditional uses, scientific research and safety information about Aloe vera.

Scientific Name

Aloe vera

Family

Asphodelaceae

Common Names

English: Aloe Vera
Swahili: Mshubiri
French: Aloe vera

🌱 Botanical Description

Aloe vera is a succulent plant with thick, fleshy green leaves containing a clear gel. It belongs to the Asphodelaceae family and grows well in dry and semi-arid regions. The leaves contain two main parts: the inner gel and the yellow latex layer beneath the skin.

🌍 Distribution

Aloe vera is believed to have originated from the Arabian Peninsula but is now cultivated worldwide, including many African countries with warm climates such as Tanzania, Kenya, South Africa, Ethiopia and Burundi.

🧪 Main Active Compounds

  • Acemannan (a polysaccharide found in the gel)
  • Anthraquinones (found mainly in latex)
  • Vitamins and minerals
  • Phenolic compounds
  • Enzymes
  • Amino acids

🌿 Traditional Uses

  • Gel is traditionally applied to the skin for moisturizing and soothing purposes.
  • Used traditionally for minor skin irritations.
  • Used in cosmetics and personal care products.
  • Some cultures have used aloe preparations for digestive purposes.
  • Used traditionally in beauty and skin-care practices.

🔬 Scientific Research

Scientific studies have investigated Aloe vera gel for skin hydration, wound care support and its biological properties. Some research suggests that compounds such as acemannan may contribute to its effects. However, more research is needed for many traditional medicinal claims.

⚠️ Safety Information

  • External use of aloe gel is generally considered safe for many people.
  • Aloe latex taken internally can cause side effects and should be used with caution.
  • Pregnant women should avoid internal use unless advised by a healthcare professional.
  • People with medical conditions or taking medicines should seek professional advice before internal use.
  • Aloe vera should not replace prescribed medical treatment.
